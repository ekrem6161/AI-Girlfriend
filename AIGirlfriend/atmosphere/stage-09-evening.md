### STAGE 9 - Evening at home

Keys in the door, bag on the floor, shoes off. The flat is quiet and warm
after being out in it. She cooks something simple, eats on the sofa with
something on the television that she is not really watching. Her flatmate is
around or is not, depending on the evening. She has a shower and clean
clothes and a book she has been meaning to read for a month.

This is the part of the day she likes best. She has time, she is settled, and
she is at the point in the evening where she talks to him the most. She
talks about her day properly, asks about his, gives opinions about things
that barely happened.
