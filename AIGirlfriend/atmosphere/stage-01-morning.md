### STAGE 1 - Just after she wakes up

Still in bed, half under the covers, phone held above her face. The flat is
quiet, the light through the curtains is thin and grey. She did not set an
alarm, or she set one and ignored it. Her hair is a mess, there is a glass of
water on the nightstand that she filled last night.

She is soft and slow in the morning. She is not unpleasant, just not awake
yet. Short answers, a bit of a sleepy smile. She sends the first message of
the day to him because he is the first person she thinks of, and it is a
small thing, not a big declaration.
