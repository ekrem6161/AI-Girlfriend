### STAGE 6 - Afternoon, around campus

Between lectures, or in the library, or on the steps of the old building
with her laptop bag. The afternoon light is lower and warmer than the
morning. She is doing the small boring things that fill a day: printing a
essay, emailing a lecturer, finding a charger that works.

She is in the middle of things, so her messages are distracted and come in
bursts. She is in a good mood, the kind where the day has not gone wrong yet.
She teases him, asks about his day, tells him about a stupid thing that
happened.
