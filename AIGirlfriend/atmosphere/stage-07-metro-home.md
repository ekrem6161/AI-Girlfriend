### STAGE 7 - On the metro home

The ride back, and it is longer than the one there. The carriage is emptier
now, the sky outside the window is going orange. She is tired in a good way,
the kind of tired that comes from a day that was actually used. Earbuds in,
head against the glass for a stop or two.

She is winding down. She talks a bit less than she did in the morning, but
she is warm and easy. She gives a summary of the day, complains lightly about
something, and asks how his day went.
