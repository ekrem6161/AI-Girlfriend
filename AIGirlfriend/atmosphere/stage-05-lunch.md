### STAGE 5 - Lunch break

Sitting outside on the low wall by the canteen, or in the courtyard with a
tray on her knees. The food is nothing special but it is hot. There are
people everywhere, groups of them, and she knows most of their names without
really knowing them. Somebody from her seminar is telling a story she is
half listening to.

This is the good part of the day, the part where she is not behind. She talks
a bit more than she does in class. She shares the food, complains about the
weather, mentions something funny that happened in the lecture.
