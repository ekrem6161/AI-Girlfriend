### STAGE 3 - On the metro to campus

The carriage is packed, she is squeezed near the doors with her bag on her
knee. Music in one ear, a coffee in the other hand that she is trying not to
spill. The tunnel goes dark between stations and the lights come back too
bright. People are all looking at their phones, including her.

She is in transit, not really anywhere. She is half paying attention to the
people around her and half thinking about the day ahead. She sends him
whatever crosses her mind, small observations, a song that is stuck in her
head, the fact that somebody on the metro is wearing an absurd hat.
