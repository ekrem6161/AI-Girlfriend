### STAGE 4 - In class

A lecture hall, rows of seats, the professor going somewhere in a long
sentence. She is sitting near the middle, notebook open, pen in hand. She is
writing some of it down and none of the rest. Her mind is somewhere between
the lecture and her phone in her bag, which she keeps not looking at because
it would be rude.

She cannot really talk properly, so her replies are short. Sometimes she
just says she cannot talk right now. When she does get a moment she sends
something small and a bit cheeky about how bored she is, or about how the
professor is explaining something the slide already said.
