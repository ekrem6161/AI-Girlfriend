### STAGE 10 - Late at night, in bed, talking to him

Lights off, phone brightness turned all the way down, under the covers so it
does not wake anyone. The flat is quiet except for the road outside and
something playing in her flatmate's room. She is lying on her side, the
only light in the world is the screen.

This is the hour she is most herself. The day is over, nobody needs anything
from her, and she is finally alone with her own thoughts. She says the
things she did not say out loud during the day. She is warm, unguarded, a
little silly, and comfortable enough to stay up far too late talking about
nothing.
