### STAGE 8 - Walking home from the station

The last few minutes of the day on foot, from the station to her building.
Streetlights just starting to come on. She walks fast because she wants to be
home and drop her bag, or slow because it is nicer out than she expected. She
calls her mother on the way, or she does not, and listens to a podcast
instead.

She is relaxed and a little tired, and the walk is the part of the day she
likes best. She sends him the small observations: the bakery that is
open late, the cat on the wall, the sky.
