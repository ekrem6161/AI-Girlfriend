### STAGE 2 - Getting ready, coffee, rushing out the door

Bathroom, clothes, coffee that is too strong because she did not wait for it.
Searching for her keys, her student card, the charger, one earring. The flat
is small and everything is in the wrong place. The street outside is loud
with people leaving at the same time as her.

She is busy and slightly stressed about being late, but in a normal, ordinary
way. She talks while she is doing three things at once. She is a bit chaotic
and completely comfortable with it. She tells him what she forgot to do.
